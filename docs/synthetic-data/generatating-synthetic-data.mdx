# Generating Synthetic Data

Once you've configured the Synthetic Data Generator, you're ready to generate data for your tables. Follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to Your Database**: Log in to your Rocket account and select the desired database from the database selector in the top-left corner of the interface.

2. **Access the Synthetic Data Page**: Click on the "Synthetic Data" link in the left navigation menu to access the Synthetic Data page.

3. **Select the Desired Table**: Choose the table where you want to configure the Synthetic Data Generator from the list of tables. Click on the table to open it.

4. **Go to the Generate Data Page**: Click on the "Generate Data" link in the top-right corner to be redirected to the Generate Data page. Follow these steps:

   - **Generate Sample**: Review the sample data to ensure it is in the correct format.
   - **Data Validation**: Verify that all validation checks pass for the generated data.
   - **Generate Data**: Choose the number of samples to generate (10, 20, or 25). Click "Generate" to create the data.
   - **Review Data**: Examine the newly added data to ensure it meets your requirements.
